1. WHAT SHRAA IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHRAA is a voice-first personal assistant for one phone (private use).
Say "Shraa suno" (like "Hey Google"). She wakes up, shows a small pop-up,
listens to ONE command, says what she is doing, does it, and goes back to rest
until the wake phrase is said again. Hindi, English and Hinglish are
understood. She speaks in a female voice ("kar rahi hu").

Online intelligence: Google Gemini API only. Without internet or a key, an
offline basic mode still handles simple commands (section 7).

2. FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wake and voice
- Wake phrase "Shraa suno" (also "Shraa suno <command>" in one sentence).
  "Shraa" alone or any other talk is ignored. The name must be the 1st or 2nd
  word. Optional "Sensitive wake word" also accepts mishearings like "Sara suno".
- Wake pop-up: small SHRAA card over any app and the lock screen; the screen
  turns on. Settings switch "Lock screen popup" turns this off while locked
  (SHRAA then still answers by voice).
- One command per wake. Optional Follow-up mode keeps listening a few seconds
  after a task so "Shraa suno" is not needed again (default OFF).
- Acknowledge first: she says "Haan, main YouTube khol rahi hu" and the task
  starts only after that sentence has finished. After success she stays quiet;
  she speaks again only on failure or when she needs a choice from you.
- Stop phrases such as "bas" end the conversation.
- Quiet listening: the recognizer's start "bip" while idle is muted (see 6).

Apps
- Open ANY installed app by name; misspelled or misheard names ("spotfy",
  "sim music") map to the closest INSTALLED app. She never suggests an app that
  is not on the phone. Hindi-script names work.
- Play a song/video (PLAY_MEDIA) or search (SEARCH_IN_APP) inside ANY installed
  app. Spotify and YouTube Music use their own links; other apps are operated
  through their own screen with Phone control (open app, tap search, type,
  press search, open first result, press Play). YouTube is used only when named
  or when no app is named.
- Play Store install (INSTALL_APP): "Play Store kholo aur <app> install karo"
  works for any app name. She opens the page (by package if the AI is sure,
  otherwise by searching the closest result), taps Install and the follow-up
  Accept / Install anyway button. Says "pehle se installed hai" if already there.
- Media control (APP_CONTROL): play, pause, stop, next, previous, forward,
  rewind, nowplaying for a connected music/video app.

Messages and calls
- Call by contact name (dialer opens unless Phone permission is granted).
- SEND_MESSAGE ("bhejo", "send karo") really sends: SMS directly with the SMS
  permission; WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram and other apps through
  Phone control (chat opened, Send tapped). Result is spoken: "Message bhej
  diya." or "Send button nahi mila, khud send dabao."
- DRAFT_MESSAGE ("open WhatsApp and type ...", "likh do") only opens the chat
  and types the text; it never sends.
- Several contacts with the same name (or one contact with several numbers):
  she reads the options ("Pehla, ..., Doosra, ...") and asks which one. Answer
  with the name or "pehla / doosra / teesra". Nothing is called until you choose.

Time, routine, phone control
- Alarms/reminders: "kal subah 6 baje mujhe utha dena" -> alarm in the phone's
  clock app (SET_ALARM) and a spoken confirmation. "roz" = daily repeat. An
  alarm more than 24 hours away becomes a calendar event (she says so).
  Reminders with text, calendar events (CALENDAR_EVENT).
- "Good morning" brief (DAILY_BRIEF): time, next alarm, calendar events of the
  next 24 h, weather (Open-Meteo, free, no key). Time and alarm always work,
  also offline. Calendar and Location permissions are optional.
- Volume (up, down, mute, unmute, max, min or percent), torch on/off, settings
  pages (wifi, bluetooth, display, sound, battery, location, apps, airplane).
- UI_CONTROL inside connected apps: back, home, recents, notifications,
  quicksettings, lock, screenshot (API 28+), scroll up/down, click <text>,
  type <text>.
- Conversation context for 3 minutes ("YouTube kholo", then "ab Arijit Singh
  ka song chalao"). The AI sees the last 6 turns and what was done.
- General questions: ANSWER_ONLY, a short spoken answer.

Connected Apps
- Settings > Connected Apps lists every installed app (icon, name, category:
  Music / Video / Games / Social / Other) with per-app switch, search, category
  filter, "Ye sab connect" / "Ye sab hatao". Default = all apps connected.
  Not connected -> "<app> SHRAA se connected nahi hai. Settings me Connected
  Apps se connect karo."
- Access levels (one-time, cover all connected apps):
    Media control = Notification access (ShraaNotificationListener)
    Phone control = Accessibility (ShraaAccessibilityService); works only inside
    connected apps (back/home/recents/notifications work anywhere).
- Android does not let one app grant another app's permissions.

Profile tab
- Screen time of the last 7 days (bar chart + average), app-wise usage (today /
  7 days), command statistics of the last 7 days (green = done, red = failed,
  success rate, most used actions) and recent commands. History is stored only
  on the phone (last 500 commands) and can be cleared. Needs Usage Access for
  screen time and app usage.

3. SCREENS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Top bar: SHRAA name (left), Settings button (right; a back arrow inside it).
- Bottom bar (exactly three buttons): Home, Profile, Background ON/OFF.
- Home: animated SHRAA orb (tap = start listening, tap again = stop), status
  label, what she heard, and a red reason line if idle listening has a problem.
- Profile: stats and history (section 2).
- Settings:
    BACKGROUND ASSISTANT  Background switch, Lock screen popup, Quiet listening,
                          Sensitive wake word, Follow-up mode
    ONLINE AI             Gemini key box, Save & Connect (tests the key), Remove
    VOICE                 Listening language (Hindi/Hinglish or English), SHRAA

    PERMISSIONS & SETUP   Microphone, Notifications, Contacts, Phone calls, SMS,

4. HOW IT WORKS (FLOW)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mic -> WakeWordEngine (short speech sessions, checks only for "Shraa suno")
    -> AssistantController.onWake()   [pop-up opens]
    -> SpeechInput (the command, listened for immediately)
    -> IntentClient (Gemini picks actions as a forced function call
       "execute_plan"; offline parser if there is no internet/key)
    -> TTS says the acknowledgement ("Haan, ...")   <-- first
    -> ActionExecutor runs the actions               <-- after speech ends
    -> History saved -> rest mode (wait for "Shraa suno")

Action types: OPEN_APP, PLAY_MEDIA, SEARCH_IN_APP, MEDIA_CONTROL, CALL,
SEND_MESSAGE, DRAFT_MESSAGE, SET_REMINDER, CALENDAR_EVENT, OPEN_SETTINGS,
SET_ALARM, VOLUME, TORCH, DAILY_BRIEF, INSTALL_APP, APP_CONTROL, UI_CONTROL,
ANSWER_ONLY, UNSUPPORTED.

Source files (com.abhirha.shraa)
  MainActivity, ShraaApplication, AssistantController, WakePopupActivity
  ai/       AiProvider, ProviderApi, PlanSchema (tool schema + system prompt),
            IntentClient, ApiKeyStore, ShraaModels
  actions/  ActionExecutor, LocalIntent (offline parser), NameMatch, DailyBrief,
            SpokenTime

5. ONLINE AI: GOOGLE GEMINI
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why Gemini: fast replies, native function calling (reliable structured output),
good Hindi/Hinglish, free tier for personal use.

  Default model     gemini-flash-latest (auto-updating alias)
  Backup models     gemini-flash-lite-latest, then gemini-3.1-flash-lite
  Endpoint          https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models
  Key               from aistudio.google.com (looks like AIza... or AQ....)

Behaviour
- The model is built in (no model field in Settings).
- 5xx / timeout: retried with a short wait, then the next backup model.
  429 or unreadable output: next model at once. Wrong key (401/403): stop.
  No internet: stop and fall back to the offline parser.
- Settings > Online AI > Save & Connect sends a tiny test request and shows OK
  with the model name, or the exact error.
- The key is stored encrypted (EncryptedSharedPreferences on API 23+; private
  preferences on API 21-22). It is never hardcoded or logged.
- Earlier versions also had other providers. Only Gemini remains; any old
  ZenMux / FreeLLMAPI key still on the phone is deleted at app start.
- Gemini free tier: limits are per project (aistudio.google.com/rate-limit) and
  Google may use free-tier content to improve its products. Do not speak
  sensitive data to SHRAA on the free tier.

6. BACKGROUND ASSISTANT (LIKE "HEY GOOGLE")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background ON starts a microphone foreground service ("SHRAA is active",
  Stop button in the notification). Nothing happens until "Shraa suno".
- WakeWordEngine runs short SpeechRecognizer sessions back to back, preferring
  on-device recognition. Heard speech is only matched against the wake phrase;
  it is not shown, stored or sent to Gemini.
- Quiet listening (default ON): the recognizer's start "bip" is muted for a
  moment around each idle session (QuietEarcon). Only streams SHRAA muted itself
  are restored; playing music is never muted and calls still ring. The bip,
  pop-up and listening animation appear only AFTER "Shraa suno".
- If idle listening has a real problem (no offline speech pack, mic busy,
  recognizer crashing) the Home screen shows the reason in red.
- Wake pop-up: closes about 1.4 s after SHRAA is back in rest mode; X or tap
  outside only closes the card, it never stops SHRAA. Without "Display over
  other apps" a full-screen notification opens the same card.

7. OFFLINE BASIC MODE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No internet, no key, or every AI call failing: LocalIntent still handles
alarms/reminders, volume, torch, open app, call, settings, media keys, play on
YouTube, time and "good morning". Anything else: she says it needs internet.
Offline AI answers would need an on-device model, which is NOT included.
Speech recognition offline needs an offline speech pack on the phone.

8. SETUP / BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the folder in Android Studio or Code on the Go; Gradle sync (9.6.1).
   See BUILD_NOTE.txt for the build configuration details.
2. Run on a real phone (emulators are poor for speech).
3. Settings > Online AI: paste the Gemini key, Save & Connect.
4. Grant permissions: Microphone (required); Notifications; Contacts, Phone,
   SMS (calls/messages); Calendar + Location (good-morning brief); Usage access
   (Profile stats); Display over other apps (wake pop-up and opening apps from
   the background); Battery unrestricted (optional, keeps listening alive).
   Optional: connect apps and enable Media control (Notification access) and
   Phone control (Accessibility) in Settings > Connected Apps.
5. Home: turn Background ON and say "Shraa suno", or tap the orb.

Release signing (optional): app/build.gradle.kts reads release.properties
(storeFile, storePassword, keyAlias, keyPassword) only if that file exists.

9. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HTTP 404 model not available   The built-in model alias was retired. Update
    the default model in ai/AiProvider.kt or use the latest app version.
HTTP 503 "high demand"         Gemini is busy; SHRAA retries and tries the
    backup models. Try again in a moment.
HTTP 429                       Free-tier rate limit; wait a little.
"AI API key galat lag rahi hai"  Re-paste the key in Settings > Online AI.
Pop-up does not appear         Allow "Display over other apps". Android 10+
    blocks background activity starts without it. SHRAA still works by voice.
She does not wake              Say "Shraa suno" at the start of the sentence,
    close to the phone; allow Battery "unrestricted"; check Background is ON;
    install an offline speech pack for the chosen language.
Bip every few seconds          Keep "Quiet listening" ON.
Opens an app but nothing plays Enable Phone control (Accessibility) and connect
    the app. Games and apps that draw their own screen cannot be operated.
Message not sent               Turn on Phone control and connect the app;
    allow SMS permission for SMS.

10. KNOWN LIMITS (ANDROID RULES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Standby keeps the microphone open (Android shows its mic indicator). Wake
  checks are local; nothing goes to Gemini before "Shraa suno" was heard.
- Wake detection uses SpeechRecognizer loops: more battery than a dedicated
  hotword engine, and some phones restart or beep sessions. Replacing it with
  a dedicated offline wake-word engine is a possible future improvement.
- Android cannot switch Wi-Fi/Bluetooth directly; SHRAA opens settings pages.
- Operating other apps works through Accessibility; apps that block screen
  access (banking) or draw their own screen (games) cannot be operated and
  SHRAA says so honestly.
- Only official Android intents and APIs: no secret control of other apps.
- Android 10+ may block opening apps from the background.

11. PRIVACY AND SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Personal-use app (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, Accessibility, Notification access).
  Do not publish it with personal keys.
- Nothing on screen is read out or stored by the Accessibility service; it only
  acts when you ask by voice, inside connected apps.
- Command history and settings stay on the phone (backup disabled).
- BEFORE PUSHING TO GITHUB: release.keystore and release.properties (keystore
  passwords) are inside the project folder and are NOT listed in .gitignore.
  Add them to .gitignore (also .gradle/ and .cg/), and never commit them.
  If they were committed before, remove them from history and make a new
  keystore. The Gemini key is not in the source code.
